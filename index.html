PHASE I: THE LOCKER ROOM

"Before we go to the pitch, let's warm up."

Answer the Coach's 3 Questions.

Question 01

Do you prefer playing sports or watching sports on TV?

Why?

Ask the student: "What is your favorite sport, and who do you usually watch or play it with?"

Question 02

Who do you think is the best football player in the world right now?

Is it Messi, Ronaldo, Vini Jr, Mbappe, or someone else?

Ask the student: "What makes them the best? Are they fast, smart, or do they score a lot of goals?"

Question 03

Why is teamwork important, not just in sports, but in life?

"Talent wins games, but teamwork wins championships."

Ask the student: "Can you give an example of teamwork in your school or job?"

FORMATION: 4-3-3

"This is an attacking formation. Very popular in modern football."

  • 4 Defenders: To protect the goal.
  • 3 Midfielders: To control the center of the pitch.
  • 3 Forwards (Attackers): Two wingers on the side and one striker to score goals.

FORMATION: 4-4-2

"This is a classic, balanced formation."

  • 4 Defenders: A solid defensive line.
  • 4 Midfielders: Good defense and good attack balance.
  • 2 Forwards (Strikers): Working together to score.

PRESENT CONTINUOUS

Affirmative

"He is running to the goal."

"They are celebrating."

Negative & Question

"She is not (isn't) passing."

"Are they winning?"
